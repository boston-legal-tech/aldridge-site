Aldridge Counsel

Boutique counsel for complex corporate matters.

About the firm

Aldridge Counsel was founded in 2008 to offer mid-market clients an alternative to big-law. We handle the same complexity, with the same depth, at a scale that allows partners to remain directly involved in every engagement.

Our practice spans M&A, private equity, employment, and trusts and estates. Our attorneys trained at major firms (WilmerHale, Goodwin, Ropes and Gray) before joining the firm. We are licensed in Massachusetts and New York.

Practice areas

Four core practice areas, served by attorneys with prior experience at major Boston and New York firms.

Corporate and M&A

We represent mid-market companies on the full lifecycle of corporate matters: formation, financing, acquisitions, and exits. Our team has handled transactions ranging from $5 million to $400 million.

Private Equity

Sponsor-side and portfolio-company representation across the Northeast mid-market. We work with sponsors on platform acquisitions, add-ons, recapitalizations, and exits.

Employment and Litigation

Employment counseling, executive agreements, and selective commercial litigation for our corporate clients. We do not handle plaintiff-side work or class actions.

Trusts and Estates

Estate planning and trust administration for our families and their related businesses. Often coordinated with our corporate work for owner-operated companies.
